# Finite future full-flow diagnostic — proposal only

No execution, instrumentation edit, or runtime authority is supplied by this review. The historical cases stay terminal and byte-exact. A new observation cannot recover their missing bind-time state retroactively.

## Smallest useful scope

Start with **one new connected full app/CLI flow**, using the current reviewed product source, including the existing stop-loop fix. Use one fresh private fixture, discovery off, virtual daemons, explicit private IPC, and two fixture loopback ports reserved once under the established ownership rules. Do not touch the old fixture ports, live services, production defaults, or protected ports. Do not retry the flow or a bind.

The flow must reach real trusted TLS readiness through the existing app/public CLI interface, retain the connected idle interval, perform one public disconnect, perform one trusted rejoin with exact public readback, and use the existing public stop/teardown path. Keep B's one-second peer-wait check and its first non-reusing bind per owned port as separately reported predicates. The current stop fix does not justify changing those historical predicates or making a latency-contract claim. No new stop timing comparison is needed.

Keep the existing finite outer limits: 60 seconds including cleanup, at most two daemon processes plus the app, at most 128 app/controller public requests, no steady-idle IPC requests, one disconnect, one rejoin. An observation-capacity limit or watchdog overrun makes the diagnostic incomplete; it does not grant a retry or a passing result. This new case would distinguish the B-style mechanism only if its decisive evidence is captured.

If the A-style retained raw accepted socket remains a separate question, it needs **one separately authorized fresh real example CLI workflow**, with its two actual TCP connections, genuine ClientHello, unpolled deadline expiry, first-eligible-poll timeout, same-driver pinned PASS/BACK recovery, and original final cleanup ordering. Keep the existing 500 ms budget, 12-second internal cap, 15-second watchdog, two-connection cap, 8192-call cap, 64-event cap, 256-trace cap and 16 KiB ClientHello cap. Preserve its original no-reuse bind predicate. This is the complete NetDriver CLI flow, not an isolated socket microtest. It is not permission to rerun A, reuse its fixed ports, or resume its other default/full/refusal cases. At most one new flow per mechanism, each with a unique archive and separately approved address allocation.

## Evidence needed before either flow is useful for causality

Prepare private instrumentation for the actual full-flow call paths. Freeze its diff and source/binary bindings before a run. The isolated Swift instrumentation/build gate remains a prerequisite; this document does not open it.

1. At socket creation, accept, any descriptor duplication/transfer, shutdown, and final close: record a socket generation plus descriptor number, owner/connection ID, local and remote tuple, address family, observed reuse/linger options, monotonic syscall entry/return brackets, and numeric return/errno captured immediately. A reused descriptor number alone is not identity. Log no input payload, TLS key bytes, credentials, or unrelated endpoint/path data. Preserve cleanup order; do not close A's accepted socket earlier as a diagnostic convenience.
2. For the **single original strict bind**: record the exact socket/options and local address, entry/return bracket, and errno before formatting or other syscalls. Keep failed-bind, lsof, TCP-state snapshot, and PID/group observations as separate events with their own brackets. Snapshots before/after bind are useful context; they must not be called the state at bind.
3. To settle the particular kernel cause, require a supported, narrowly filtered, loss-accounted observation of that bind's rejection decision: matching local PCB/namespace identity, tuple/state, reuse flags, and the selected rejection branch, correlated with the bind return. Read the actual kernel build identity and matching primary source. A read-only trace/debug interface may supply this **only if already available under separate authorization without security/global-default changes**. Do not enable SIP exceptions, install a kernel, alter signing/firewall defaults, or assume a restricted trace facility will work. If no such observation is available, stop at the observation-design boundary. More delayed netstat/lsof cannot meet this requirement.

Use a bounded event buffer, for example 512 lifecycle records and 64 bind/decision records per new flow, with an explicit loss/overflow flag. Precompute any tuple filters from the owned fixture and track only its accepted/outbound sockets and exact/wildcard local conflicts. If a kernel interface cannot restrict or safely filter its output, it is unsuitable. The public report should contain only the selected owned/conflict fields, without private global paths or unfiltered captures.

## Decision rules

- A bind-time conflict tied to a still-owned listener or duplicated accepted descriptor, after its intended release boundary, supports an ownership defect in that **new** flow. Source must explain the surviving owner before assigning P1/P2.
- A bind-time conflict tied to an ownerless local closing PCB or TIME_WAIT endpoint, with its exact rejection rule and no matching live owner in the available lifecycle evidence, supports a kernel-endpoint/oracle explanation for that **new** failure. A LAST_ACK result would distinguish A's candidate from the unsupported client-TIME_WAIT explanation. A local 65202-style TIME_WAIT result would distinguish B's candidate.
- A different conflict or namespace rejection selects that different cause. A later matching row, missing lifecycle events, trace loss, lack of deployed-source mapping, or an unobserved decision leaves cause unresolved. Do not fill the gap with a generic platform rule.
- A successful new strict bind reports that new result only. It does not prove why an old bind failed. No second bind, alternative reuse policy, changed timing, or substituted port is used to rescue a failed predicate. Product-listener restart capability remains unmeasured unless separately requested as a real public flow.

There is no promise of reproduction or universal process absence. The proposal makes one finite observation per selected mechanism, with explicit criteria for a causal result and an explicit incomplete outcome.
